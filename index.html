Karnataka Introduction

Karnataka is one of the most beautiful states in southern India. It is famous for its ancient temples, cascading waterfalls, pristine beaches, misty hill stations, and vibrant modern cities. Bengaluru, the state capital, is widely recognized as the Silicon Valley of India.

Coorg (Kodagu)

Renowned as the Scotland of India, Coorg is celebrated for its lush coffee estates, fragrant spice plantations, forested hills, and pleasant year-round climate.

Historical Places

Architectural Wonder

Gol Gumbaz

Located in Vijayapura, Gol Gumbaz features one of the largest unsupported domes ever built and houses an acoustic whispering gallery where every sound echoes multiple times.

Hoysala Marvel

Belur Chennakeshava Temple

A masterpiece of 12th-century Hoysala artistry, showcasing intricate soapstone filigree work, bracket figures, and narrative reliefs along the Yagachi River.

Ancient Capital

Waterfalls

Jog Falls

Formed by the Sharavathi River falling in four distinct cascades—Raja, Roarer, Rocket, and Rani—making it one of India's tallest un-tiered plunge waterfalls.

Waterfalls
