Welcome to the Canteen Queue Center! This web application is designed to help users manage and track canteen queues efficiently. You can view the current queue status, join the queue, and receive notifications when it's your turn.

Features:

  • View current queue status
  • Join the queue
  • Receive notifications when it's your turn

To get started, please log in or sign up to access the queue management features.

Thank you for using the Canteen Queue Center!
